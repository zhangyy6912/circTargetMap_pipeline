Circular RNAs (circRNAs) are covalently closed RNA molecules generated primarily through back-splicing of precursor mRNAs. Owing to their exceptional stability and widespread expression, circRNAs have emerged as important regulators of gene expression and key contributors to disease pathogenesis. Although advances in high-throughput sequencing have enabled the identification of numerous circRNAs, their interacting RNA targets remain largely unexplored. To address this gap, we developed CircTargetMap, a computational framework for globally mapping circRNA–target RNA interactions using large-scale RNA interactome sequencing datasets. Here, we present an easy-to-use and reproducible protocol for identifying candidate RNA targets associated with circRNAs.
